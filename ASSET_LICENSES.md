# Asset licensing and provenance

Last reviewed: 2026-08-22

This document records the licensing and provenance status of non-code assets
associated with this repository. It is intentionally conservative: an entry
marked **TO BE CONFIRMED** does not grant permission to use or redistribute
that asset.

## Scope of the repository license

The root [MIT License](LICENSE) covers the source code and its software
documentation. It does not automatically cover model weights, seismic data,
manual interpretations, derived scientific data products, or the manuscript
PDF. Those assets are governed by the terms listed below.

## Status definitions

- **Confirmed**: the rightsholder and applicable license have been documented.
- **TO BE CONFIRMED**: the source or permission is incomplete; do not publicly
  redistribute the asset until the missing information is resolved.
- **Not distributed**: the asset is required or referenced but is not included
  in the public repository.

## Information still required for the seismic data

Before publishing any SEG-Y file or image containing seismic amplitudes,
record all of the following:

- official dataset or survey name and identifier;
- original provider and rightsholder;
- authoritative download URL or DOI;
- version and access date;
- exact license or terms-of-use URL;
- whether redistribution of the complete file, a spatial subset, processed
  values, and rendered images is permitted;
- required attribution wording;
- survey coordinate reference system, axis units, and SEG-Y header convention;
  and
- any confidentiality, embargo, or commercial-use restrictions.

Public availability or free download access must not be treated as permission
to redistribute the data.

## Information still required for the model weights

The model-specific technical record is in [MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md).
Before releasing `model_real.pth`, additionally confirm:

- the legal rightsholder for the weights;
- authorization from relevant authors or institutions;
- whether the training-data terms allow release of trained weights;
- the exact model-weight license and version;
- permissions for use, modification, fine-tuning, commercial use, and
  redistribution; and
- required attribution and citation wording.

If the weights are intentionally released under the same MIT terms as the
code, that decision must be stated explicitly here after authorization; it
must not be inferred from the root `LICENSE` file.

## Derived products

A derived artifact should record its generating code, source assets,
parameters, software version, and checksums. A new license may be assigned only
when it is compatible with the terms of all upstream assets. In particular,
an overlay image containing seismic amplitudes may have different release
constraints from a geometry-only surface mesh.
